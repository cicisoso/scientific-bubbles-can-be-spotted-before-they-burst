# Science Research Article requirements (digest; verify on the live pages before upload)

Sources: science.org "Instructions for authors of new research articles" and "Instructions for preparing an initial manuscript" (search snippets, 2026-09-19; the pages block automated fetches), plus a third-party digest (manusights.com, 2026). Items marked (verify) differ between sources.

- Article type: Research Article (Reports are shorter: ~2,500 words, 4 display items).
- Title: <= 135 characters including spaces (verify). One-sentence summary: <= 125 characters, submitted with the manuscript.
- Abstract: preferred <= 125 words (250 words allowed at submission), single paragraph, no citations.
- Length: submitted Research Articles 6,000-8,000 words with 5-8 figures (official snippet); word count includes references, notes and captions (historic guidance: "up to ~8,000 words including references, notes and captions"). Aim for <= 8,000 all-inclusive.
- Sections (required, labelled): Abstract; Introduction; Results (subheadings allowed); Discussion; Materials and Methods (a summary in the main text is acceptable with the detailed methods in the Supplementary Materials); References and Notes; Acknowledgments (Funding, Author contributions, Competing interests, Data and materials availability); Supplementary Materials list.
- References: numbered in order of citation, in parentheses in the text (1, 2); Science style "A. B. Author, Title. Journal Abbrev. Volume, pages (Year)."; guideline ~40 references for Research Articles (verify); Supplementary Materials references continue the main numbering.
- Figures: single column 8.5 cm (5.5 cm?) / double column 17.5 cm (verify; use <= 17.8 cm); fonts Helvetica/Arial 6-8 pt; 300 dpi (1,200 dpi line art) at final size; vector PDF/EPS preferred; multi-panel figures count as one display item; figures cited in order.
- Supplementary Materials: single PDF: Materials and Methods, Supplementary Text, figs. S1-S..., tables S1-S..., references; separate data files (Data S1...) allowed.
- Initial submission: single PDF containing text, figures with legends and tables, double-spaced with line numbers; separate Supplementary Materials PDF; cover letter (optional but customary; name suggested reviewers, exclusions, related manuscripts); competing-interests form (AAAS) completed by all authors at submission.
- Policy: authorship (all authors approve), data and code availability (deposit before publication; Dryad/Zenodo etc.), AI use disclosed in the acknowledgments/methods (Science permits AI-assisted writing with disclosure; AI cannot be an author; AI-generated images not allowed).
